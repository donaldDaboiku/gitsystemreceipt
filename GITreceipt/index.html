Still Managing Receipts Manually?

Handwritten receipts get lost. Payment records sit in notebooks. Staff spend time rewriting the same details, and the business cannot show a clean history when a customer asks.

GITSYSTEM Receipt keeps each receipt on the phone, numbers it, and lets a paid plan print it, save a PDF, or send it on WhatsApp. The dashboard totals what was collected.

What you can do today

Instant receipts

Enter the customer, items, and amount. The receipt number is created for you.

One professional layout

Every receipt uses the same clear layout, with your business name once you add it.

Your logo

Starter and above can place a business logo on the receipt.

Receipt history

Search past receipts and see totals for today, this month, or all time.

PDF, print, WhatsApp

Paid plans can print, save a PDF or image, and share on WhatsApp.

Sales snapshot

The dashboard groups collections by category. Backup export is included.

Customer lists, extra templates, staff logins, and multiple locations are not available yet.

Frequently asked questions

What is GITSYSTEM Receipt?

It is a receipt app from GreenPasture Integrated Tech System. You create a receipt, keep the history, and share it when your plan allows.

Can I use it for free?

Yes. Free includes 5 receipts each calendar month, one business, and on-screen history.

Can I add my business logo?

Yes, from the Starter plan upward.

Can I print receipts and send them on WhatsApp?

Print, PDF, and WhatsApp are included from Starter.

Can I upgrade?

Yes. Choose a plan in the app and pay with Paystack. The plan starts only after Paystack confirms the payment.

What happens when a subscription expires?

The account returns to Free: 5 receipts a month, without PDF, print, WhatsApp, or a new logo upload.

Can I cancel?

A paid period runs until its end date. It does not renew by itself. You can ask the admin to suspend an account.

Can several staff use one account?

Not yet. Each business uses one login.
